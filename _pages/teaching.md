## Deep Learning for Time Series Forecasting
**Intelligent Earth CDT, University of Oxford** · January 2025 and January 2026

Lab session in the Time Series Analysis and Signal Processing course of the UKRI AI Centre for Doctoral Training in AI for the Environment (Intelligent Earth). Probabilistic forecasting with deep autoregressive models, with hands-on PyTorch implementations of DeepAR and ConvTrans. [Course materials](https://github.com/george-nigmatulin/Deep-Learning-for-Time-Series-Forecasting)

## Volatility Forecasting with Economic Networks
**The Oxford Summer School in Economic Networks** · 25 June 2026

Tutorial taught with Yaxuan Kong at the summer school of the Institute for New Economic Thinking at the Oxford Martin School and the Mathematical Institute. Participants build correlation networks from daily futures on equity indices, currencies, rates and commodities, then forecast volatility with a graph convolutional network. [Course materials](https://github.com/george-nigmatulin/economic-networks-summer-school)
